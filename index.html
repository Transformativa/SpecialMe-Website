The problem

Care for a child with special needs runs on paperwork.

Reports arrive as scanned PDFs that nobody can search.

Every new provider asks you to tell the whole story again.

Deadlines, doses and appointments live in five different places.

Sharing with grandparents or a teacher means forwarding everything or nothing.

How it works

Three steps from paper to a plan you can use.

  1. 1

    Upload

    Add an IEP, an evaluation, or an OT, PT or speech report. Scans and photos are fine.

  2. 2

    Review

    SpecialMe suggests goals, services and dates. You check each one and fix anything that is off.

  3. 3

    Share

    Invite family, teachers and therapists. You pick which sections each person can see, and you can remove them any time.

Who it is for

Children now, and adults who still need the same support.

Children with IEPs

Each child gets a profile with school goals, therapy reports, medications and a care team. Siblings get their own profiles.

Adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities

Families and caregivers share medications, schedules and appointments, with consent and legal authority recorded for each adult.

Privacy

Your child’s records are not our business model.

  • No ads, no selling dataFamilies pay for the app. Nothing else pays for it.
  • You decide who sees whatShare sections one person at a time and take access back whenever you want.
  • Export and deleteDownload your records or delete them, including after you cancel.
  • Built to HIPAA standardsWe are designing for HIPAA-level protection and will sign agreements with our vendors before launch.

AI reads your documents to suggest what goes in your child’s profile. It can make mistakes, which is why you review every item.

Questions

Before you sign up.

Is SpecialMe available now?

No. We are building it and collecting families for the first trial. Joining the trial list is free and does not commit you to anything.

What do trial families get?

Early access, and a say in what we build. We will share any early pricing by email before anyone is asked to pay.

What will it cost?

Pricing is not final. It will be a simple yearly or monthly plan per person, and invited family, teachers and therapists will be able to view for free.

Which documents does it read?

IEPs, evaluations, and OT, PT and speech reports, including scans. You always review what it finds before it is saved.

Is it only for children?

No. Adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities will get their own profiles for medications, schedules and appointments.

Is this medical or legal advice?

No. SpecialMe helps you organize information and understand documents. It does not replace your child’s doctors, therapists, school team or an advocate.
