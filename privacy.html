SpecialMe

Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026. This covers the SpecialMe launch website only, not the app, which is not yet available. The app will have its own privacy policy before anyone uses it.

Who we are

SpecialMe is operated by Transformativa LLC. You can reach us at contact@gettransformativa.com.

What we collect

When you join the trial list we collect:

  • your email address,
  • your first name, if you give it,
  • the type of person you say you are (parent, caregiver, professional or other), and
  • the date and time you signed up, and that you agreed to be emailed.

Please do not send us health, school or other personal details about your child or anyone else through the form. We do not ask for them.

How we use it

We use it to email you about the SpecialMe trial, demo and launch, and to understand who is interested. We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

Where it is kept

Sign-ups are stored with our database provider, Supabase, in the United States. Only our team can read them. The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies and does not run analytics. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may see your device’s IP address when the page loads.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove you or delete your information at any time by emailing contact@gettransformativa.com. We will do it promptly. We keep sign-ups until you ask us to remove them or until we no longer need them for the trial and launch.

Children

This website is for parents, caregivers and professionals. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
