Demo

Write a vision for your child, with your child.

Answer a few questions and SpecialMe drafts two short pages for an IEP meeting: a Parent Vision and, in your child’s voice, a Child’s Vision. You can edit both before you save them.

Everything stays on your device. Your answers are used only on this page. They are not uploaded or saved, and they are gone when you close it.

This demo does not use AI. It places your answers into sentence templates, so please read and edit the draft until it sounds like you.

Use a first name or nickname only. Please leave out last names, school names, ID numbers and diagnoses you are not ready to share.
